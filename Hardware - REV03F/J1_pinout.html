J1 pinout · CBM 4032 IEEE-488 HAT REV03F

The 40-pin Raspberry Pi header and what each pin carries on this board, taken from the REV03F PCB netlist. Levels on the Pi side are 3.3 V. Every bus line is non-inverting: GPIO low = bus low = asserted.

Bench supply: +5 V goes to pin 2 or 4, GND to pin 6. On the underside of a stacking header the columns are mirrored, so check the underside view before clipping on.

Top view (component side)

Looking down on the HAT as it sits on the Pi. Pin 1 has the square pad.

Underside view (where the pins stick out)

The HAT turned over left to right. The odd and even columns swap sides; pin 1 stays at the top.

Pin table

Direction is seen from the Pi. "Board part" is the translator or pull resistor on that pin. J2 is the PET edge-connector contact the signal reaches.
